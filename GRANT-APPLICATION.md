## What We're Building

mirv is the first fully autonomous cross-chain liquidity mirroring system built natively on Uniswap V4. Custom hooks deployed on Ethereum, Base, and BNB Chain detect real-time depth and price imbalances across sister pools, then use Hyperlane to coordinate automatic rebalancing — no human keepers, no downtime, no manual intervention required.

The user-facing product is a public ERC-4626 vault (deployed on Base) that accepts a single deposit and automatically distributes the LP position across all three mirrored pools. An AI agent swarm — built on LangGraph with Claude 4 — polls every pool every 45 seconds, calculates optimal rebalances, and dispatches cross-chain adjustments through the hook system. Users receive `mirvETH-USDC` vault tokens and earn meaningfully higher APY than any single-chain LP position, generated by capturing arb convergence, tighter effective spreads, and dynamically optimized fee tiers across chains.

## Why It Matters for the Uniswap Ecosystem

Today's cross-chain liquidity is fragmented. The same pair trades on Ethereum, Base, and BNB at independent depths and prices. Each fragment continuously bleeds value through arbitrage that flows out of the ecosystem. mirv closes this loop: rather than reacting to arbitrage after the fact, the system prevents it by keeping sister pools synchronized in near-real-time.

Uniswap V4 hooks make this possible for the first time. The singleton PoolManager, flash accounting, and per-hook lifecycle callbacks (`afterSwap`, `afterAddLiquidity`, `afterRemoveLiquidity`) give us the precision to intercept pool events and trigger cross-chain coordination within seconds. This is a fundamentally new DeFi primitive that V4's architecture uniquely enables.

## Unique Technical Contributions

**First agentic hook system at scale.** AI agents don't just observe — they instruct hooks via Hyperlane, creating an adaptive liquidity organism that evolves with market conditions rather than following static rules.

**Pair-agnostic factory.** Any trading pair meeting a $500k TVL and $100k daily volume threshold across all three chains can be automatically mirrored via `MirrorFactory.sol` — the system is designed to scale with the ecosystem, not just serve two hardcoded pairs.

**Honest performance fee design.** The vault charges 15% exclusively on the yield differential (mirrored APY minus baseline single-chain APY). LPs pay nothing for normal returns — the protocol only earns when mirroring demonstrably adds value.

**Open-source reference architecture.** Every hook, vault, and agent will be fully open-sourced. We intend for mirv to become the canonical example of an AI-agentic, cross-chain V4 hook — the DeFi primitive other builders fork and extend.
